## 1.0.0

- Initial release.
- `Cloth` widget: renders any child as fabric taped to the wall by its top edge.
- 3D Verlet mass-spring simulation with strain limiting, curvature resistance
  and progressive tearing of the anchors.
- Lit, depth-sorted mesh rendering with diffuse and specular passes.
- `ClothController` for pulling the pins, knocking the sheet off and resetting.
